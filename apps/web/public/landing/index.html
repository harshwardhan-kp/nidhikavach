how it works

three sources, one truth

Every FDR field is checked three ways — what the institution declared, what the scan shows, what the bank confirms — and rolled into one trust score. Maker ≠ checker approvals and a hash-chained audit trail make fraud visible, not just recorded.

Two readers on every scan

Azure Document Intelligence and gpt-6.1-terra read each FDR independently; where they disagree, the field is flagged instead of guessed.

[real]
Pasted amounts get caught

A compression check finds the region that was edited after the scan was saved, draws a box on it and shows a heatmap; the model gives a second opinion.

[real]
The bank answers in seconds

Account Aggregator and NeSL-shaped confirmation; banks off the network send a letter that a second officer must confirm.

No maturity goes unwatched

A daily pass re-derives every deposit's status, sends T-90 / 60 / 30 / 7 reminders and escalates overdue renewals one level a week.
